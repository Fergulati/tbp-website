## DEEP RESEARCH
### Tokenizing the Real World: How Public Blockchains Are Rewriting the Rules of Asset Ownership

**The Moment Has Arrived**

Numbers attached to real world asset tokenization are staggering. The on chain RWA market excluding stablecoins sits at approximately \$32 billion as of mid June 2026. That is up from roughly \$12 billion in early 2025 representing 200% year over year growth. Citi published its Tokenization 2030 report this month projecting the total tokenized asset market will reach \$5.5 trillion by 2030. McKinsey offers a more conservative figure of \$2 trillion. Ark Invest sees \$11 trillion. The Boston Consulting Group projected \$16.1 trillion by 2030 in its foundational report. Even the most conservative estimate represents a fundamental restructuring of how financial assets are created and transferred.

The IMF has characterized RWA tokenization as a structural reconfiguration of capital markets. 2026 is widely described by analysts as the breakout year for the sector. The evidence this week supports that characterization.

**What Real World Asset Tokenization Actually Is**

Real world assets are traditional financial instruments brought onto a blockchain. Think US Treasury bonds. Think real estate. Think corporate debt. Think private equity funds. When these assets exist on a blockchain they can be transferred 24 hours a day seven days a week. Settlement compresses from days to seconds. Fractional ownership becomes possible. Access expands to investors who were previously excluded by capital minimums or geography. Yield becomes programmable.

The asset moves but the ownership record lives on an immutable ledger. Anyone with permission can verify the holder in real time. Compliance rules get embedded directly into the asset rather than enforced by a separate intermediary.

**Which Blockchains Are Winning**

The race is not winner take all. It is multi chain with a clear hierarchy.

Ethereum holds approximately 45% of on chain RWA value. This reflects deep liquidity and the institutional default position. BlackRock's BUIDL fund is anchored on Ethereum along with most tokenized Treasury and private credit products. Arbitrum and Base absorb additional tokenized activity at lower cost.

Provenance Blockchain is the stealth second largest chain holding roughly 27% of on chain RWA value. It was purpose built for regulated financial services and dominates in corporate lending and mortgage backed tokenization. Its market leadership exists almost entirely below the public radar.

Stellar landed the most consequential institutional deal of mid 2026. The DTCC and the Stellar Development Foundation announced on May 27 that DTC's tokenization service will connect with Stellar as its first public blockchain partner. DTC custodies over \$114 trillion in total assets. The initial scope covers Russell 1000 stocks and major ETFs and US Treasuries. The Stellar network was chosen for its enterprise compliance features its built in asset clawback capabilities and its high transaction throughput.

Solana is the fastest growing institutional chain. Tokenized RWA value on Solana surpassed \$2 billion in 2026 representing roughly 9% of the market. BlackRock BUIDL and Franklin Templeton BENJI both deployed on Solana. The network holds 97% of all cumulative tokenized equity spot trading volume. Major issuers now deploy products across eight or nine chains simultaneously rather than committing to a single network.

**What Is Being Tokenized**

Tokenized US Treasuries sit at \$14.8 billion as of June 10. There are now 82 separate products and 65,729 individual holders. The four dominant issuers are Circle with roughly \$2.9 billion then Ondo Finance at \$2.8 billion then BlackRock BUIDL at \$2.5 billion then Franklin Templeton BENJI at \$1.98 billion. This is the most institutionally mature category.

Tokenized private credit holds approximately \$18.9 billion in active on chain loans by the RWA.xyz methodology. Key platforms include Maple Finance and Centrifuge and Goldfinch. Yields in this category range from 8% to 15% annually. Apollo and BlackRock and WisdomTree are entering this space from the traditional side.

Tokenized equities is the fastest emerging category. Ondo Global Markets launched in January 2026 with 200 tokenized US stocks and ETFs on Solana. By mid 2026 the platform offers 430 assets across three chains. The SEC's approval of Nasdaq and NYSE tokenized equity trading rules in March and April 2026 creates the structural foundation for this category to scale.

Tokenized gold crossed approximately \$5.5 billion in market cap. Trading volume in Q1 2026 surpassed all of 2025's combined volume. The sector is growing five times faster than physical gold ownership.

Tokenized real estate remains mostly in pilot phase on chain. The category is projected to exceed \$1.4 trillion as programs scale. RealT has tokenized over \$150 million in multifamily real estate on Ethereum.

**The Firms Building This Infrastructure**

BlackRock BUIDL is now the largest tokenized Treasury product globally at \$2.5 billion across eight blockchains: Ethereum and Arbitrum and Aptos and Avalanche and Optimism and Polygon and Solana and BNB Chain. BUIDL was approved as derivatives trading collateral on Binance in May 2026. BlackRock also filed for two additional tokenized fund structures with the SEC this quarter.

Ondo Finance operates at a combined \$2.8 billion across its OUSG and USDY products. The SEC closed a multi year investigation into Ondo with no charges in early 2026. The company has since expanded aggressively into tokenized equities via Ondo Global Markets. A notable milestone: Ondo executed the first live cross border tokenized Treasury redemption on the XRP Ledger in partnership with JPMorgan and Mastercard and Ripple.

Franklin Templeton BENJI holds \$1.98 billion across eight public blockchains. BENJI is the first US registered mutual fund to use a public blockchain for share ownership records. The fund celebrated its five year anniversary in 2026. Investor count grew over 140% from April 2024 to March 2026.

Securitize is the platform that manages over \$4 billion in tokenized assets for BlackRock and Apollo and KKR and Hamilton Lane. The company reported 841% year over year revenue growth through September 2025. It cleared a key regulatory hurdle in early June 2026 to list on the NYSE via a SPAC merger at a \$1.25 billion valuation.

**The Risks That Still Exist**

Oracle dependency is the deepest structural risk. RWAs require off chain data feeds to bring real world prices on chain. For thinly traded tokenized assets price discovery is fragile. A corrupted oracle can be catastrophic and the vulnerability is inherent to the model regardless of which oracle network is used.

Regulatory fragmentation creates cross border transfer problems. The SEC and CFTC issued a landmark joint classification framework on March 17 2026 creating five asset categories. The EU's MiCA framework is in force but differs in its classification logic. A tokenized asset that is fully compliant in one jurisdiction may be non transferable to investors in another. China's 2026 regulatory notice opened a legal pathway for domestic asset tokenization while keeping it separate from cryptocurrency activity. Three major jurisdictions now have three different frameworks for the same assets.

Liquidity remains thin. Despite \$32 billion on chain most tokenized assets show thin secondary market trading and long holding periods. Tokenization does not automatically create a liquid secondary market. The underlying assets are often inherently illiquid. The Financial Stability Board flagged potential systemic risk from liquidity mismatches in tokenized funds in a prior report.

Legal wrapper risk is the most underappreciated threat. The architecture connecting a token to its underlying asset is the critical link. A flawed legal wrapper means token holders may have no enforceable claim in a default or insolvency even if the blockchain record is perfectly accurate.

**The Bottom Line**

Tokenization of real world assets is not a future possibility. It is live infrastructure being built by the same firms that manage the world's largest pools of capital. The DTCC chose a public blockchain. BlackRock is deploying across eight chains simultaneously. Franklin Templeton has been doing this for five years. A nationally chartered US bank just launched a stablecoin on Solana. The convergence of regulatory clarity in the US and EU and institutional scale deployment and infrastructure buildout from firms like DTCC and Securitize and BlackRock makes 2026 the year the question changes from "will this happen" to "how fast and on which chains."
