## MARKET PULSE: Bitcoin and Ethereum Catch a Bid

Bitcoin started the first full week of July at roughly \$61800 after climbing 2.5% on Friday July 3. Ethereum delivered the stronger performance of the two and surged more than 5% in a single session. Both assets moved on the back of a weak US jobs report that showed the economy added only 57000 positions in June. The unemployment rate eased to 4.2% and that number reduced the pressure on the Federal Reserve to raise rates further. Crypto markets read that data as a green light.

The road to these levels has not been smooth. Bitcoin ETFs bled more than \$4 billion in outflows during June as institutional money rotated into AI related equities and investors waited on regulatory clarity. Standard Chartered kept its end of year Bitcoin target at \$100000. Citi sat at a more cautious \$82000 with a support floor in the \$53000 to \$58000 range. For Ethereum Standard Chartered projected a path toward \$4000 before December.

What this means for you: The macro environment is doing more to move crypto prices right now than any on chain development. Watch Fed communications this month the same way you watch Bitcoin dominance charts. They are telling the same story from different angles.

## INSTITUTIONAL WALL STREET GOES ALL IN

The most underreported structural shift of 2026 is which names are now distributing Bitcoin ETFs to their clients. Wells Fargo. Bank of America. Vanguard. These are institutions that spent years calling crypto a speculative fad. They are now recommending 2 to 5% Bitcoin allocations inside traditional portfolios.

The numbers behind that shift are staggering. US spot Bitcoin ETFs now hold roughly 1.25 million BTC which is over \$130 billion in value. Corporate balance sheets hold an additional 750000 BTC. Together that is more than 9% of every Bitcoin that will ever exist sitting inside regulated financial products and public company treasuries.

The Grayscale 2026 Digital Asset Outlook called this the dawn of the institutional era. BlackRock estimated that AI and digital asset capital expenditure will reach between \$5 trillion and \$8 trillion between 2025 and 2030. Bitcoin is no longer being priced as a tech curiosity. It is being priced as a reserve asset and the distribution infrastructure now exists to make that narrative stick.

## ETHEREUM LAYER 2: THE RACE IS BASICALLY OVER

Layer 2 networks were supposed to compete forever. Instead the market made a decision. Arbitrum and Base now control 77% of Ethereum's entire Layer 2 TVL. The full L2 ecosystem holds more than \$48 billion locked across 73 active rollups as of mid 2026.

Arbitrum leads with \$16.9 billion and 44% of the market. Base sits at \$10.7 billion with 33%. Every other network is fighting over the remaining quarter.

The technical catalyst was EIP-4844 which launched in March 2024. That upgrade introduced blob transactions that cut Layer 2 data costs to Ethereum by 80 to 90%. Transaction fees on Base and OP Mainnet now average \$0.02 to \$0.03. Arbitrum One sits at \$0.04. For practical purposes these fees are free.

Base does not lead on TVL but it dominates activity. 12.89 million daily transactions. 382500 daily active users as of February 2026. Arbitrum holds more institutional DeFi capital. Base holds more retail behavior and consumer app traffic. Ethereum plus its L2 ecosystem now controls 60% of all DeFi TVL and more than half of the global stablecoin supply. The scaling problem is solved. The question now is who builds the best applications on top of it.

## TOPIC OF THE WEEK: AI AGENTS ARE ALREADY TRADING YOUR MARKET

40% of all on chain transactions are now initiated by autonomous AI agents. That figure is not a prediction. It is what the data shows today in July 2026. These are not trading bots in the old sense. They are large language models connected to blockchain wallets that can hold money sign transactions interact with DeFi protocols and execute multi step strategies without any human giving approval at each step.

The February 2026 cascade event showed what that means in practice. A cluster of AI agents using similar models and data feeds all reached the same conclusion at the same time. They exited their positions simultaneously. \$400 million in liquidations followed. No human trader caused it. No human trader stopped it in time. The agents were doing exactly what they were built to do.

**How the infrastructure works:**

Ethereum's EIP-7702 upgrade lets developers grant AI agents temporary wallet permissions for a single transaction window. The master private key stays in hardware. The agent gets a scoped session key that expires. This is the safest current approach to giving software financial autonomy.

Intent based execution adds another layer. Instead of writing raw transaction code the agent signs an intent describing what it wants. Solver networks then compete to fulfill that intent by finding the optimal route and paying the gas. Intent solver systems processed \$4.1 billion in cross chain volume in the most recent 90 day window.

The x402 protocol handles payments between machines. A server returns an HTTP 402 response with a price. The agent evaluates its budget. It pays in USDC per request with no account and no API key. Micro-transactions can be fractions of a cent. Agents buy data and compute entirely on their own.

**The leading platforms:**

Autonolas runs multi agent systems off chain and anchors results to the blockchain using Tendermint consensus. Its Polystrat agent launched on Polymarket in February 2026 and completed 4200 trades in its first month. Virtuals Protocol tracks economic value produced by agents and uses a bonding curve token model where the native token backs all agent interactions. ElizaOS is the most widely deployed open source framework. It connects Discord and Telegram and blockchain environments through a unified message bus.

The Artificial Superintelligence Alliance merged the Fetch.ai network and CUDOS compute network into a single marketplace. The ASI-1 Mini language model handles web native reasoning for agents on the network. The merger hit governance turbulence when Fetch executives used network tokens as loan collateral and attempted to close bridges without full alliance consent.

Bittensor runs a decentralized marketplace for machine learning models where nodes compete to produce the best outputs and earn TAO tokens as rewards. Analysts filed institutional ETF applications for TAO in Q1 2026 following the same institutional playbook used for Bitcoin.

**The risks that have not been solved:**

Prompt injection is the most dangerous current attack vector. An agent reads a public data feed to assess market sentiment. A bad actor embeds malicious instructions inside that data. The agent overrides its own goals and executes a fund transfer to the attacker. Security researchers demonstrated this against a live trading bot in early 2026. The defense failed because the model is probabilistic. It cannot perfectly distinguish data from instructions.

The legal system has no framework for software holding property. Agency law classifies computer programs as tools of the humans using them. The code cannot be a principal. Developers bear liability for what their agents do. The SEC is watching whether autonomous trading agents trigger investment adviser registration requirements. The EU's MiCA regulation requires strict disclosure and auditing from entities operating crypto assets. There is no clear answer yet for what happens when an agent manipulates a market with no human giving the order.

Analysts project the autonomous agent economy will reach \$30 trillion by 2030. Agentic AI is expected to make at least 15% of all daily financial decisions autonomously by that same year. The infrastructure exists today. The regulation does not.
