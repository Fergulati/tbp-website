Strategy Buys 34,164 BTC for \$2.54 Billion in Largest Purchase Since 2024

Strategy completed its largest single Bitcoin purchase since 2024 on April 20. The company acquired 34164 BTC at an average price of \$74395 per coin. Total holdings now stand at 815061 BTC.

The total acquisition cost across all of Strategy's purchases to date is approximately \$61.56 billion. The April 20 purchase ranks as the third largest single BTC acquisition in the company's history. Strategy funded the purchase through its STRC preferred stock program which continues to provide significant capital capacity.

Strategy also made two earlier purchases in April. On April 7 the company bought 4871 BTC for \$329.9 million at an average of \$67718. On April 13 it acquired 13927 BTC for approximately \$1 billion at \$71902 per coin.

Policy

100+ Crypto Firms Push Senate to Act on the CLARITY Act Before July Deadline

A coalition of more than 100 crypto companies is pressing the Senate Banking Committee to advance the CLARITY Act. Coinbase and Ripple lead the effort. The bill would establish a federal framework that gives digital assets a clear regulatory home in US law.

A breakthrough on stablecoin yield rules reached on April 14 removed the last major obstacle blocking Senate progress. The Banking Committee must complete its markup before the end of April to meet the July legislative target. The White House has signaled it wants the bill signed before the midterm election cycle picks up pace.

The SEC and CFTC issued a joint interpretation in March 2026 clarifying how federal securities laws apply to crypto assets. On April 13 the SEC published guidance allowing certain blockchain interface operators to avoid broker-dealer registration under specified conditions. The regulatory momentum in Washington is the strongest it has been since the 2014 Bitcoin foundation era.

Infrastructure

Charles Schwab Launches Spot Bitcoin and Ethereum Trading for 38 Million Clients

Charles Schwab launched direct spot Bitcoin and Ethereum trading this month through its Charles Schwab Premier Bank unit. The service covers 38 million clients who hold \$11.9 trillion in combined client assets.

Trading costs are set at 75 basis points per trade which puts Schwab among the lowest cost crypto brokerage options available. Clients can view crypto holdings alongside stocks and bonds in a single account view across Schwab.com and Schwab Mobile and the thinkorswim platform.

This launch carries structural significance. Schwab is not a crypto native company. Its entry embeds digital asset access into the brokerage infrastructure that institutional allocators already use. Future phases will add more cryptocurrencies and deposit and withdrawal transfer capabilities.

Infrastructure

Chainlink Data Infrastructure Goes Live on AWS Marketplace for Enterprise Builders

Amazon Web Services added three Chainlink oracle services to its marketplace on April 24. Chainlink Data Feeds and Data Streams and Proof of Reserve are now available to the millions of developers and hundreds of thousands of businesses that use AWS infrastructure.

Data Feeds deliver decentralized price and market data aggregated from multiple independent node operators. Data Streams provide cryptographically signed real time data for faster market settlement. Proof of Reserve enables onchain verification of the reserves backing stablecoins and tokenized assets.

The integration connects traditional cloud infrastructure directly to smart contracts on blockchain networks. For the tokenized asset market this means enterprise developers can now build institutional grade onchain applications without leaving the AWS development environment they already use.

◆ Deep Research Topic

Original Analysis · April 2026

The RWA Tokenization Explosion: Who Actually Wins?

The tokenized real world asset market crossed \$27.6 billion in April 2026.
That number is both impressive and misleading. Understanding the difference tells you where real value is being created.

What RWA Tokenization Actually Is

RWA tokenization creates a digital token on a blockchain that represents a claim on a real world asset. Common assets today include US Treasury bonds and private credit loans and real estate and commodities. The token itself does not own the asset directly. A legal structure called a Special Purpose Vehicle holds the asset. The SPV gives token holders a legal claim on what the underlying asset produces or is worth.

Without that SPV legal structure the token is worthless as a financial instrument. The blockchain is a record of who holds a claim. The legal wrapper is what makes the claim enforceable. Most retail investors and some builders miss this distinction entirely.

Where the Market Stands Today

The total onchain RWA market excluding stablecoins reached approximately \$27.6 billion in April 2026. Growth from \$6.5 billion in early 2025 represents a fourfold expansion in roughly 14 months.

Tokenized US Treasuries lead the market at approximately \$14 billion. Securitize controls 42 percent of that segment. BlackRock and Ondo Finance and Hashnote and Franklin Templeton collectively hold 80 percent of tokenized Treasury supply. Private credit sits at \$3.2 billion. That is up 180 percent from \$1.14 billion at the start of 2025. Tokenized real estate accounts for \$2.5 billion. Tokenized commodities sit at \$1.2 billion with gold as the dominant product through Paxos Gold and Tether Gold.

The Institutional Entry That Changes the Equation

JPMorgan rebranded its blockchain division as Kinexys in 2025. The platform has processed over \$3 trillion in cumulative settlement volume. Average daily volumes now exceed several billion dollars. In December 2025 JPMorgan launched MONY as the first tokenized money market fund on Ethereum seeded with \$100 million of the bank's own capital.

BlackRock's BUIDL fund is now live across Ethereum and Solana and Polygon and BNB Chain. As of late 2025 BUIDL manages \$2.3 billion and is the largest tokenized institutional fund in the world. Goldman Sachs and BNY Mellon are building tokenized liquidity funds on both permissioned and public networks. JPMorgan CEO Jamie Dimon stated publicly in April 2026 that JPMorgan must move faster on tokenization because blockchain based settlement directly threatens traditional bank fee income.

Nomura's 2026 Digital Assets Institutional Investor Survey found that nearly 80 percent of institutions plan to allocate 2 to 5 percent of AUM to digital assets. Over two thirds of those institutions specifically target DeFi mechanisms including staking and lending and tokenized assets. These are production commitments from the largest capital pools in the world.

The Liquidity Paradox: A \$27 Billion Market That Barely Trades

Here is the detail most market commentary skips. A \$27.6 billion market sounds highly liquid. It is not. Research on residential real estate tokens found average turnover of once per year per token. Traditional REIT shares typically turn over multiple times per year. A 2026 Canton tokenization report found 1 to 3 percent pricing gaps for identical assets listed on different chains. Moving capital between chains costs an additional 2 to 5 percent in friction. A 53.8 percent majority of RWA issuers surveyed by Brickken in early 2026 said their primary goal is capital formation rather than liquidity creation.

The RWA market lacks a unified secondary exchange. Investors must check multiple platforms with different prices. Liquidity is siloed across decentralized exchanges and alternative trading systems and private broker-dealer networks. Most tokenized assets are classified legally as securities and may only be sold to accredited investors or those who have passed KYC and AML checks. That legal constraint permanently limits the buyer pool.

◆ Proprietary Frame

The standard debate asks whether assets will go onchain. That question is settled. \$27.6 billion is already there. The correct question is who captures value from the infrastructure that makes it possible. The answer points to two layers. The legal wrapper layer where Securitize controls 42 percent market share in tokenized Treasuries. The oracle and data layer where Chainlink just entered AWS Marketplace reaching millions of enterprise developers. These are the railroad operators. In the 1800s you wanted to own the railroad not the cattle. The rails capture the margin. The assets riding the rails generate the revenue.

That is the economic engine driving every major Wall Street firm into this space right now.

Three Headline Insights with Action Implications

◆

**Tokenized US Treasuries are the only RWA category with genuine institutional grade liquidity today.**
Private credit and real estate tokens carry private placement risk despite their blockchain wrapper. Allocators seeking RWA exposure should weight toward the Treasury segment until secondary market infrastructure matures for other asset classes.

◆

**Wall Street is using tokenization to reduce its own post trade costs first.**
Democratization of asset access is a marketing narrative. The primary driver is \$900 million to \$5 billion in addressable annual settlement friction. Builders and advisors should orient their roadmaps toward the institutional cost reduction use case rather than retail access stories.

◆

**The platform capturing value in RWA tokenization is not the asset issuer or the underlying blockchain.**
It is the legal infrastructure layer represented by Securitize at 42 percent Treasury market share and the oracle layer represented by Chainlink entering the AWS enterprise ecosystem. These businesses compound over time. Asset issuers compete on yield. Infrastructure operators compound on volume.

Key Risks to Monitor

- Smart contract bugs can destroy token value with no recourse from the underlying asset structure.
- Oracle failures can trigger improper liquidations or allow undercollateralized borrowing across DeFi integrations.
- Redemption delays built into SPV legal structures mean exits from tokenized positions may take days or weeks rather than seconds.
- Securities classification permanently limits buyer pools to accredited investors in most jurisdictions narrowing secondary market depth.
- Cross chain fragmentation creates 1 to 3 percent pricing gaps and 2 to 5 percent friction costs for capital moving between networks where the same tokenized asset is listed.

What This Means for Fergulati Consulting Clients

Clients asking about RWA exposure need to understand that current tokenized RWA yields come entirely from the underlying asset. The blockchain layer adds settlement efficiency and programmability. Those benefits are real. They do not justify paying a price premium over the underlying asset itself.

The structural play is in infrastructure. Compliance platforms and oracle networks are the compounding businesses in this space. Asset issuers compete on yield. Infrastructure operators compound on volume. The total addressable market for settlement infrastructure grows as the RWA market grows regardless of which specific assets dominate.

The CLARITY Act moving toward a Senate markup before July changes the risk profile for US based clients. Regulatory clarity reduces the compliance cost of entering this space and opens the buyer pool for tokenized securities beyond current accredited investor restrictions. That is a meaningful catalyst for secondary market liquidity in 2026 and 2027.
