## This Week in Crypto

### Bitcoin Holds the \$64K Line

Bitcoin started this week at \$64,199. The price dipped below \$63,000 early in the trading week as rising tensions between the United States and Iran sent crude oil above \$80 per barrel. Risk assets sold off broadly. Softer inflation data later in the week helped Bitcoin recover to around \$64,753. Key resistance sits at \$67,500. A clean break above that level would flip the short term outlook to bullish. Institutional ETF flows remain a headwind with over \$4 billion in net Bitcoin ETF outflows over the past 30 days. Watch that \$65,631 level closely. It represents the 50 month exponential moving average and has acted as a magnet for price this week.

---

### The CLARITY Act Is In Trouble

The Digital Asset Market Clarity Act is stalled in the Senate. Three major disputes are blocking the seven to nine Democratic votes needed to clear the 60 vote filibuster threshold. The first dispute centers on insider trading and ethics disclosures. President Trump's financial disclosure revealed approximately \$1.4 billion in cryptocurrency related income during 2025. That disclosure has made bipartisan compromise more difficult. The Senate returned from recess on July 13 leaving roughly three weeks before August recess. Prediction market odds of passage before August recess have fallen to about 43%. Trump took to Truth Social on July 13 to personally call on the Senate to pass the bill. Senator Lummis had previously committed to a floor vote in July. That commitment is now under pressure. A July 17 hearing was framed by analysts as the decisive moment for the bill's 2026 fate.

---

### Robinhood Chain Goes Live

Robinhood launched the public mainnet of Robinhood Chain on July 1. The network is an Ethereum Layer 2 built on Arbitrum technology. It went live with tokenized stocks including NVDA and AAPL that can be traded 24 hours a day. Those Stock Tokens are available in over 120 countries. The network logged over 17 million transactions in its first week. It attracted nearly 350,000 addresses and over \$1 billion in decentralized exchange volume. Chainlink serves as the official oracle infrastructure for the entire network. An important caveat: Stock Tokens are structured as tokenized debt instruments redeemable for cash. They are not direct equity ownership. Despite launching with a DeFi focus a report from CoinDesk noted that memecoins and stablecoins have dominated early activity on the chain rather than the featured tokenized equities.

---

### Ethereum's Pectra Upgrade Is Live

Ethereum's Pectra upgrade is now live on mainnet. Pectra combines the Prague execution layer and the Electra consensus layer changes into a single upgrade. Key improvements include account abstraction enhancements and increased validator stake limits. Blob throughput has also been raised. Ethereum's next scheduled hard fork is Glamsterdam which targets base layer throughput and is set for the second half of 2026. Vitalik Buterin also published a "Lean Ethereum" roadmap this month covering quantum safety and privacy and scalability improvements planned through 2029.

### ETF Flows: Ethereum Surging as Bitcoin Stabilizes

Crypto ETFs ended an eight week outflow streak earlier this month. The sector attracted \$281.8 million in net inflows to break that streak. Bitcoin funds took in \$197.4 million while Ethereum products added \$84.4 million. The recovery has gathered pace. Spot Ethereum ETFs saw \$96 million in net inflows in just the first three trading days of this week alone. That total already surpassed the prior week's full figure of \$84 million. Institutional interest in Ethereum is clearly picking up. BlackRock's BUIDL tokenized treasury fund crossed \$2.87 billion in total multi chain assets under management. Avalanche now hosts the second largest BUIDL allocation after Ethereum.

---

### Security Alert: Allbridge Core Exploited for \$1.65 Million

Cross chain bridge protocol Allbridge Core suffered an exploit this week resulting in approximately \$1.65 million in stolen funds. Attackers moved the funds from Solana to Ethereum. Bridge exploits remain one of the highest risk categories in DeFi. Users with funds on lesser audited cross chain bridges should review their exposure.

## Deep Dive: Real World Assets Are Rewriting the Rules of Finance

This week's deep research topic is real world asset tokenization. It may be the single most consequential trend in blockchain right now.

### What Is RWA Tokenization?

Real world asset tokenization is the process of representing ownership of a physical or financial asset as a token on a blockchain. Those assets include US Treasury bills and bonds and real estate and private credit and equities and commodities like gold. The blockchain token tracks ownership and can be transferred and traded and used as collateral without requiring traditional financial infrastructure.

### The Market Is Larger Than Most People Think

The total value of tokenized real world assets on public blockchains reached approximately \$32 billion by June 2026. That figure nearly tripled from \$11.8 billion just one year prior. A broader estimate that includes all tokenized products across platforms puts the figure closer to \$60 billion. The most important number is the trajectory. The market grew 589% since 2025. Tokenized bonds and money market funds led the sector growing 83% and adding \$6.5 billion in value. Boston Consulting Group and Standard Chartered project the market could reach \$16 trillion by 2030. That would represent nearly 10% of global GDP.

### BlackRock Is Leading the Institutional Wave

BlackRock launched the BUIDL fund in March 2024 on Ethereum. BUIDL is a tokenized money market fund that invests in short dated US Treasury bills and overnight repurchase agreements. Each BUIDL token represents one dollar of exposure to government debt. Holders earn the prevailing Treasury bill yield directly in their wallet through a daily rebase mechanism. By mid 2026 BUIDL had grown to approximately \$2.5 billion in assets under management. It now operates across nine blockchain networks. It became tradeable on Uniswap in February 2026. It was accepted as collateral on Binance in November 2025. BUIDL is the largest tokenized treasury fund on the planet. Securitize serves as the transfer agent and tokenization infrastructure partner. The minimum entry is \$5 million which keeps the product in the institutional lane for now.

### Robinhood Chain Is Bridging Equities and DeFi

Robinhood Chain is the most visible example of tokenized equities entering mainstream DeFi. Its Stock Tokens let users in over 120 countries trade major US stocks around the clock. Those tokens can be deposited into lending protocols and used as collateral across the broader DeFi ecosystem. This creates something genuinely new. A share of NVIDIA becomes a composable financial primitive that can earn yield and serve as collateral and be traded at 2am on a Sunday. The launch of Robinhood Earn allows US users to earn approximately 7% APY on the USDG stablecoin through the Morpho lending protocol. This is an early but meaningful example of a regulated brokerage building natively inside DeFi.

### Real Estate Tokenization Lags Behind

Tokenized bonds and treasuries are scaling fast. Real estate is moving slower. The story of tokenized real estate sounds compelling: fractional ownership of commercial properties and 24 hour liquidity and global investor access. The reality is more complicated. Most jurisdictions still require a traditional title transfer for actual change of ownership. The legal complexity of real estate does not disappear when you attach a token to it. Institutional adoption in the real estate category remains limited. That will likely change as regulatory frameworks catch up but it is not a near term catalyst.

### The Risks Are Real

Liquidity is the biggest structural risk. Many RWA tokens have low trading volumes and limited investor participation. Liquidity can fragment across platforms and blockchains creating isolated pools that are difficult to exit during stress. The cross chain interoperability problem compounds this. A token on Ethereum cannot natively interact with one on Stellar or Polygon. Chainlink's Cross Chain Interoperability Protocol is the primary technical solution being deployed but it adds infrastructure complexity. Regulatory uncertainty is also significant. Legal frameworks vary widely across jurisdictions. Questions of title and smart contract enforceability and cross border recognition remain unresolved in most markets. The GENIUS Act signed into law this year requires foreign stablecoin issuers to complete OCC registration by 2028 but broader RWA regulation is still being written.

### What Comes Next

The infrastructure is now largely proven. The central challenge for RWA tokenization from 2026 to 2030 is not technology. It is regulatory clarity and institutional discipline and secondary market liquidity. When those three conditions are met the growth could be extremely fast. A market at \$32 billion today on a path to \$16 trillion by 2030 implies growth of roughly 500 times. Even if that projection is overly optimistic by 80% the remaining market expansion would still be one of the largest capital market stories in history. For clients in crypto consulting the key question is not whether to pay attention to RWAs. The key question is which protocols and asset categories will win the race to become the default infrastructure for tokenized finance.
